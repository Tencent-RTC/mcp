Tencent Cloud Real-Time Communication (TRTC) offers two primary billing options: **Prepaid packages** and **Pay-as-you-go**.
- **Prepaid Packages:** TRTC launched prepaid packages on **March 7, 2023 (UTC+8),** unlocking different value-added service features for different applications (SDKAppID). Call monthly packages, Conference monthly packages, Live monthly packages, and RTC-Engine monthly packages are provided, and you can choose an appropriate prepaid package for your business scenarios.

   > **Note:**
   > 
   > - Monthly package subscriptions support automatic renewal. By turning on **Automatic renewal**, you are agreeing to the recurring subscription renewal of the Services. 
   > - For your convenience, TRTC will be automatically deducting the relevant renewal fees from your account, credit card, debit card or through any other payment methods that you have previously provided to us in the signing up of your account or purchasing of the services. You can cancel and opt-out of such automatic renewal at any time. In that case, payment will be terminated following the expiry of that current subscription term in which you opt out of the renewal.

- **Pay-as-you-go:** If your usage cannot be covered by a prepaid package or exceeds the remaining prepaid quota, billing switches to pay-as-you-go based on actual usage. Once the monthly free quota is exhausted and pay-as-you-go is enabled, the service seamlessly transitions to pay-as-you-go billing without interruption.

   **The fee breakdown is as follows:**
