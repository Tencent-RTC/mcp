All Tencent Cloud accounts (UIN) that have activated the TRTC monthly package (including Call, Conference, Live, RTC-Engine, Conversational AI) can receive a free time package of 10,000 minutes each month within a year. A maximum of one free time package can be received per month, and the usage generated by all TRTC (including Call, Conference, Live, RTC-Engine,Conversational AI) can be deducted.

> **Notes on free time package:**
> 
> - The remaining free minutes for each month will expire on the last day of the current cycle and cannot be used in the next cycle.
> For example, if you receive the free minutes on November 15, 2022, they would be valid from November 15, 2022 to December 14, 2022.
> 
> You can check the validity of your free minutes in [Package Management](https://console.trtc.io/package) of the TRTC console.
> 
> - When the monthly 10,000-minute free usage quota is exhausted, the following deduction order applies:
> - If valid package minutes remain (monthly packages or RTC Engine Top-Up Packages), those minutes will be consumed first.
> - If no packages are available:
> - **Pay-as-you-go enabled**: The service automatically switches to pay-as-you-go billing with no interruption.
> - **Pay-as-you-go not enabled**: The service becomes **unavailable**. To restore access, either purchase a package or enable pay-as-you-go billing. Service resumes immediately upon restoration.

> **Note：**
> For UINs registered on or after **Beijing Time July 25, 2024 (inclusive)**, the free usage package can be used to offset minutes generated by Audio/Video Calls, Interactive Live Streaming, Cloud Recording, Stream Mixing & Transcoding, Recording Delivery to Tencent Cloud, Recording Delivery to Third Party, Web Page Recording, Live Video Streaming Audience, AI Real-Time Conversation, Voice-to-Text, AI Real-Time Translation, and Real-Time Speech Synthesis.
>
