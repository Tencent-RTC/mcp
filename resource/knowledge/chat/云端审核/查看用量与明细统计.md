> **Note:**
> This feature is currently available to whitelisted users only.
> 

Go to the [Chat Console > Cloud audit](https://console.trtc.io/chat/cloud-audit-setting), and click **Review Details**.

## Review Details

View detailed records of past recognition events. Use the filter bar to enter your search criteria and narrow down the results. You can access up to one month of recognition history.

## View Review Statistics

### Review Count Statistics

Review statistical data on the total number of recognition events.

### Review Type Statistics

Analyze statistics for different types of recognition events.

### Review User Statistics

View statistics on users who participated in recognition events.

### Violating User Leaderboard

Access the **Violating User Leaderboard** to identify and directly mute users who have violated rules.

When muting a user, select the **mute scenario** and specify the **mute time**.

For users who are already muted, you can perform the **Unmute** operation and choose the scenario for unmuting.

To view the list of muted or unmuted users, click **Mute Management**.

### Keyword interception ranking

View the historical leaderboard for keyword blocking events.
