WhatsApp Clone is a project aimed at cloning WhatsApp, offering powerful chat and high-quality audio and video call features. It utilizes Tencent's ChatUIkit and CallKit components to achieve a smooth chatting experience and stable, clear audio and video calls. The project emphasizes user data security, employs advanced encryption technology, and is cross-platform, providing users with a convenient, efficient, and secure instant messaging experience.

## WhatsApp Clone Feature Characteristics
| #### <strong>Full Platform Support</strong><br>Supports over 20,000 cross-platform devices, including iOS, Android, Web, and Flutter. | #### <strong>Single/Group Chat</strong><br>Supports personal and group calls, and various message types such as text, image, audio, and video messages. | #### <strong>Session Management</strong><br>View information about unread messages, latest, pinned, or deleted chats. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #### <strong>Audio/Video Call</strong><br>Supports high-quality, low-latency audio and video calls. | #### <strong>Group Call</strong><br>Supports multi-user online audio and video calls, and allows inviting others to join during the call. | #### <strong>Group Management</strong><br>Create groups, manage group members, and customize avatars. |
| #### Call Notifications and Offline Push<br>If the app is not in the foreground, push notifications will be sent. | #### <strong>Virtual Background</strong><br>During a video call, you can set a blurry background or image background. | #### <strong>Floating Window</strong><br>The call page can be displayed as a floating window. |
| #### AI-powered Noise Cancellation<br>Remove background noise from your audio and video calls using AI technology. | #### <strong>Weak Network Call Lagging Optimization</strong><br>Reduce lagging rate and loading time in weak network environments. | #### <strong>Global Deployment</strong><br>Providing services to users in over 200 countries and regions. Compliant with the <General Data Protection Regulation> (GDPR). |
